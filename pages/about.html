About LogicLab

A futuristic, fully interactive digital electronics playground.

🎓

Built for DTM

Aligned with Diploma in Technology digital electronics topics: gate behaviour, truth tables, boolean algebra, De Morgan's theorem, half adders and universal gates.

⚙️

Real, not decorative

The circuit builder runs an actual logic engine. Every gate is evaluated from the graph of wires you draw — signals genuinely travel through the simulation.

💻

Runs anywhere

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No installation, no sign-up — open it in any browser. Work is saved locally on your machine.

🗺️

Roadmap

Coming next: flip-flops, half/full adders, logic challenges (build XOR from NANDs), experiment mode with guided DTM labs, and a minimap for big circuits.
