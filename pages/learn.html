Learn Digital Logic

Every logic gate with its symbol, boolean expression and truth table — ready for DTM revision.

De Morgan's Theorems. (A · B)′ = A′ + B′ and (A + B)′ = A′ · B′. This means every NAND can behave like an OR, and every NOR can behave like an AND — that's why NAND and NOR are called universal gates.
Try it yourself. Open the Circuit Builder, drop two switches into an AND gate, wire it to an LED, and press ▶ Run.
